# Working loop

Work only inside `implementations/qrnn`, preserve the full objective, and use this loop:

1. Sync `0-plan.md` with actual files, builds, declarations, and audit findings.
2. Select the first unfinished stage.
3. Refresh `goal-1/[INDEX]-[SHORTHAND].md` using the applicable sections of
   `implementations/BUILD-PLAN.md`, then take actions that advance the stage.
   Keep data definitions below proof layers, audits in diagnostic leaves, and
   generic calculus independent of quaternion-specific consumers.
4. Check touched modules with focused Lean builds and necessary adjacent
   consumers; use full builds when public APIs/configuration change. Inspect
   axioms and proof holes; do not equate numerical checks with proof.
5. Record material findings in the paper map/audit and update plan facts,
   decisions, stage status, and actual completion signals.
6. Continue through stages toward the original objective, adapting
   tactics while preserving its scope and constraints.
7. If a session ends mid-goal, leave a brief state/next-action/blocker note.

Prioritize meaningful verified results. Keep records concise and local to this
folder. A definition is not derivative correctness, a moment identity is not
convergence, and algebra is not evidence of empirical speech-recognition gains.
Match every completion claim to observed evidence.
